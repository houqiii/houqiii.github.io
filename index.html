About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), advised by Prof. Lan Zhang. I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science from Lanzhou University in 2024.

My research centers on reliable intent realization for agents in the physical world: grounding large language models so they can turn high-level human intent into dependable, verifiable actions in real, physical environments.

IoT-Brain: Grounding LLMs for Semantic-Spatial Sensor Scheduling

Zhaomeng Zhou, Lan Zhang, Junyang Wang, Mu Yuan, Junda Lin, Jinke Song

ACM International Conference on Mobile Computing and Networking (MobiCom), 2026 · CCF-A

Also presented at the ACM ANAI Workshop @ MobiCom 2025

A verify-before-commit spatial trajectory graph that grounds LLMs in the what-to-sense / when-to-sense decision for large-scale sensor networks. Our TopoSense benchmark, built on the real topological environment of USTC's High-Tech (Gaoxin) Campus, is open-sourced and integrated into Microsoft's System Intelligence Benchmark.
